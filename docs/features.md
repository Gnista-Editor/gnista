# What Ember does

What works today, in a terminal (`ember`) or a window (`ember-gui`). Where it differs from Emacs is in [differences.md](differences.md), and what is still to come in [plan.md](plan.md).

- Buffers.
- Windows (`C-x 2`, `C-x 3`, `C-x o`, `C-x 0`, `C-x 1`, resizing with `C-x ^`, `C-x }`, `C-x {`, `C-x +` and the mouse, `display-buffer` and the `C-x 4` commands, `C-M-v`, `C-x <left>` and `C-x <right>` through the buffers a window showed, window parameters, dedicated windows, and window configurations with `save-window-excursion`).
- Frames (`C-x 5 2`, `C-x 5 o`, `C-x 5 0` and the other `C-x 5` commands, each frame a native window in the GUI).
- Files (backups, auto-saving and `recover-this-file`, `revert-buffer` and `C-x x g`, `auto-revert-mode`, asking before editing or saving a file changed on disk, DOS and Mac line ends, files in other codings (UTF-16, the ISO 8859 and Windows sets, Shift_JIS, EUC-JP, ISO-2022-JP, GBK, Big5, EUC-KR, found from cookies and the bytes), `C-x RET f`, `C-x RET r` and `C-x RET c`, `describe-coding-system` on `C-h C`, `prefer-coding-system`, `save-some-buffers` and asking about each file on `C-x C-c`, `copy-file`, `rename-file`, `delete-file` to the trash, `make-directory`, `read-only-mode` on `C-x C-q`, `find-alternate-file` on `C-x C-v`, `rename-visited-file`, recent files with `recentf-mode` and `recentf-open`, and `find-file-at-point`, `insert-file` on `C-x i`, `write-region`, `append-to-file`, and lock files).
- Dired (`C-x d`, `C-x C-j`: visiting, marking, copying, renaming and deleting files, `g`, `s`, hiding details), with dired-aux's commands: links (`S`, `Y`, `H`), renaming and copying by regexp (`% R`, `% C` and the link ones), `% u` and `% l`, `% g` to mark files by what they hold, `!` and `&` with `*` and `?`, `y`, `M`, `O`, `G`, `T`, `Z` to compress and unpack, `A` to search the marked files into `*xref*` and `Q` to replace in them; dired-x's `dired-omit-mode` (`C-x M-o`) and `F`; and wdired, `C-x C-q`, to rename files and point links elsewhere by editing the listing (`C-c C-c` to finish, `C-c ESC` or `C-c C-k` to take it back).
- Projects (`C-x p f` to find a file in the Git tree, `C-x p p`, `C-x p D`, `C-x p b`, `C-x p k`).
- Questions (`yes-or-no-p`, `y-or-n-p`) and timers.
- An agent (`M-x agent`: talk to Claude in an Org buffer while it calls commands as tools, asking before it writes files or quits, each turn one undo step).
- Emacs movement and editing keys (`M-m`, `M-\`, `M-SPC`, `M-^`, `C-x C-o`, `M-z`, `C-q`, `C-S-DEL`, `M-g g`, `C-x =`, `M-=`, `M-@`, `M-h`, `C-M-o`, sentences with `M-a`, `M-e` and `M-k`, `sort-lines`, `reverse-region`, `delete-duplicate-lines`, `keep-lines`, `flush-lines` and `how-many`, registers (`C-x r SPC`, `C-x r j`, `C-x r s`, `C-x r i`) and rectangles (`C-x r k`, `C-x r y`, `C-x r t`), `M-/` (`dabbrev-expand`), abbrevs (`abbrev-mode`, `C-x a g`, `C-x '`), `M-s o` (`occur`), pages (`C-x ]`, `C-x [`, `C-x l`), `C-M-t`, `C-M-h`, `C-x n d`, `C-l` and `M-r` cycling as `recenter-top-bottom` and `move-to-window-line-top-bottom`, the global mark ring (`C-x C-SPC`), `undo-only`, `ESC ESC ESC`, the buffer list (`C-x C-b`, a tabulated list sorted with `S`, titled in the header line), `kill-some-buffers` and `kill-matching-buffers`, highlighting by regexp (`M-s h r`, `M-s h .`), bookmarks (`C-x r m`, `C-x r b`, `C-x r l`, in Emacs's file format), `whitespace-cleanup`, `view-mode` and `view-file`, `M-x diff`, `diff-buffer-with-file` and `diff-mode`, `tabify`, `center-line` and `append-to-buffer`, changing case with `M-u`, `M-l`, `M-c`, `C-x C-u` and `C-x C-l`, paragraphs with `M-{` and `M-}`, defuns with `C-M-a` and `C-M-e` (by the syntax tree in tree-sitter modes), balanced expressions with `C-M-f`, `C-M-b`, `C-M-n`, `C-M-p`, `C-M-u`, `C-M-d`, `C-M-k`, `C-M-SPC` and `M-(`, as each mode's syntax table says).
- Filling (`M-q`, `C-u M-q` to justify, `fill-region`, `C-x f`, `C-x .`, `auto-fill-mode`).
- Matching parens shown (`show-paren-mode`, on by default) and typed in pairs (`electric-pair-mode`).
- Line numbers (`display-line-numbers-mode`, `global-display-line-numbers-mode`).
- History and places kept between sessions (`savehist-mode`, `save-place-mode`).
- A shell in a buffer (`M-x shell`, on comint: `M-p`, `M-n`, `M-r` searching the history with isearch, `C-c M-r`, the shell's history file, `C-c C-c`, `C-c C-o`, the directory followed, passwords read hidden with `read-passwd`).
- Shell commands (`M-!`, `M-|`, `M-&`).
- Going to a definition by name (`M-x imenu`, from the syntax tree in tree-sitter modes, Python's included, and the headings in Org).
- Compiling and grepping in the background (`M-x compile`, `M-x grep`, `recompile`, `C-x p c`) and visiting each message's place (`next-error` on `M-g n`, `M-g p`).
- Finding a regexp in a project (`C-x p g`, into `*xref*`).
- Comments (`M-;`, `C-x C-;`, `C-x ;`, `M-j`).
- Indentation (TAB as the mode says, `C-M-\`, `C-x TAB`, RET and closing braces reindenting in code modes).
- The message log (`C-h e` shows `*Messages*`).
- The active region with shift selection (transient-mark-mode and shift-select-mode, both on).
- Prefix arguments (`C-u`, `M-<digit>`, `C--`).
- Mouse wheel and trackpad scrolling of the window under the pointer.
- Clicks.
- Drags.
- Double and triple clicks and shift-click as Emacs mouse keys (`<mouse-1>`, `<drag-mouse-1>`, `<double-mouse-1>`).
- Long lines continued by default.
- `truncate-lines`, `visual-line-mode` and `global-visual-line-mode`.
- Spell checking with ispell, aspell or hunspell: `M-$` (`ispell-word`, corrections in `*Choices*`), `flyspell-mode` (checks words as you type, underlining misspellings and doubled words; `C-.`/`M-TAB` cycle corrections, `C-;` corrects the misspelling before point, `C-,` goes to the next, `C-c $` offers corrections), `flyspell-prog-mode`, `flyspell-buffer` and `flyspell-region`.
- `subword-mode` (the word commands stop inside CamelCase names) and `superword-mode` (a symbol is one word), and their global modes.
- The kill ring with `M-y` (choosing a kill from the minibuffer when not after a yank).
- The mark ring (`C-u C-SPC`).
- Undo as in Emacs (undoable, with `undo-redo`, text property changes undone, `buffer-undo-list` read, set and bound, `with-silent-modifications`).
- Keyboard macros (`F3`, `F4`, `C-x (`, `C-x )`, `C-x e`).
- Recursive edits (`C-M-c`, `C-]`, `top-level`).
- The keys typed lately (`C-h l`).
- Characters typed with `C-x 8` through `key-translation-map`.
- Keys bound to keys as keyboard macros.
- `sit-for` and `read-event` with a timeout.
- Repeat (`C-x z`).
- Documented variables with buffer-local values (`C-h v`).
- Hooks.
- Major and minor modes with their own keymaps (`C-h m`).
- Choosing the major mode from the file name.
- `text-mode`.
- `overwrite-mode` and `delete-selection-mode`.
- Header lines (`header-line-format`).
- Margins (`left-margin-width`, `(margin left-margin)` display specs) and fringes in the GUI (continuation arrows, `(left-fringe BITMAP)` specs, `define-fringe-bitmap`, `indicate-empty-lines`, `indicate-buffer-boundaries`, `fringe-indicator-alist`).
- The overlay arrow (`overlay-arrow-position`, a triangle in the fringe or `=>` on a terminal, marking `next-error`'s message).
- Fonts as values (`font-spec`, `font-get`, `font-xlfd-name`, `face-font`, `font-at`, `font-family-list`, `list-fonts`, `x-list-fonts`, `char-displayable-p`).
- Images (`(image ...)` display specs on text, overlays, strings and in the margins, PNG, JPEG, GIF and SVG, `create-image`, `insert-image`, `put-image`, `insert-sliced-image`, `image-size`, `image-increase-size` and `image-rotate` on `i +` and `i r`, and `image-mode` for visited image files: drawn in the GUI, rows as tall as their images, and shown as their text in the terminal, as Emacs's terminals show them).
- The bell (`ding`, `visible-bell`, the system beep on macOS).
- Text properties.
- Overlays and faces with Emacs's attributes and colour names (`describe-face`, `list-faces-display`, `list-colors-display`, anonymous faces in `face` properties).
- Faces of other sizes and proportional fonts (`:height`, `:family`, `face-remapping-alist`, `C-x C-+`, `C-x C--` and `C-x C-0` for `text-scale-adjust`, `variable-pitch-mode`).
- With the layout's positions for scripts (`posn-at-point`, `pos-visible-in-window-p`, `window-end`, `window-text-pixel-size`).
- Bidirectional text (Hebrew, Arabic and mixed lines reordered as Emacs's bidi.c does, with paired brackets and mirroring, right-to-left paragraphs from the window's right edge, `bidi-paragraph-direction`, `current-bidi-paragraph-direction`, `bidi-resolved-levels`, `move-point-visually`, and `<left>` and `<right>` as `left-char` and `right-char`, by the paragraph's direction or with `visual-order-cursor-movement`).
- Invisible text and folding (`outline-mode`, `outline-minor-mode`), and hiding blocks of code and comments (`hs-minor-mode`: `C-c @ C-h`, `C-c @ C-s`, `C-c @ C-c`, `C-c @ C-M-h`, `C-c @ C-l`).
- Org IDs (`org-id-get-create`, `org-id-copy`, `org-id-goto`, and `[[id:...]]` links followed with `C-c C-o`), and link types for packages (`linkSetParameters`, as `org-link-set-parameters`).
- Org mode (`org-mode`: folding, structure editing, TODO states (with fast-access keys, `C-c C-t t`, and changes logged as `TODO(t!)` says, into a LOGBOOK drawer with `org-log-into-drawer`; `org-todo-keyword-faces`), tags (`org-tag-faces`), checkboxes, timestamps, links, tables, properties (`C-c C-x p`), refiling (`C-c C-w`, to the headings `org-refile-targets` names in this file or others, by outline path with `org-refile-use-outline-path`), narrowing (`C-x n s`), blocks (`C-c C-,`, and `<s TAB` once `ember.require("org-tempo")`), tangling (`C-c C-v t`), the week agenda and TODO list (`org-agenda`), capture (`org-capture`, to a file, a heading, an outline path or a date tree by day, month or ISO week, with `%^g`, `%a` and `%i`), `org-indent-mode` (text indented under its headings as it shows, `org-startup-indented` or `#+STARTUP: indent`) with `org-hide-leading-stars`, `org-ellipsis` and `org-return-follows-link`).
- Narrowing (`C-x n n`, `C-x n w`).
- Tabs (`tab-bar-mode`: `C-x t 2`, `C-x t o`, `C-<tab>`, `C-x t 0`, `C-x t u`, `C-x t r`, `C-x t m`, `C-x t RET`, `C-x t b`, `C-x t f`, `C-x t d`, `tab-list`, clicks on the tab bar, and `tab-bar-history-mode` with `C-c <left>` and `C-c <right>`).
- Sessions (`desktop-save-mode`: buffers, their modes and places, `file-name-history` and the windows saved on exit and while idle, and read back at start; `desktop-save`, `desktop-read`, `desktop-clear`, `desktop-revert`, with a lock file as Emacs's).
- Transient menus for packages (`transient.ts`: `transientDefinePrefix`, suffixes that stay or leave, switches and options read with `transientArgs`, menus within menus, `C-g` and `C-q`).
- Keys that can follow a prefix (`which-key-mode`), and `.editorconfig` files (`editorconfig-mode`).
- Splitting the frame's root window (`C-x w 2`, `C-x w 3`).
- Ember.app opened from Finder takes its PATH from the login shell, as exec-path-from-shell gives Emacs.app.
- Language servers (`M-x eglot`, `eglot-ensure`: definitions on `M-.`, references on `M-?`, back with `M-,`, completion on `C-M-i`, diagnostics underlined with `flymake-goto-next-error`, what point is on after a pause, `eglot-rename`, `eglot-format-buffer`, `eglot-code-actions`), and `xref-backend-functions` and `completion-at-point-functions` for other packages.
- Themes (`load-theme`, `enable-theme`, `disable-theme`, `custom-enabled-themes`): a theme is a package, NAME-theme, along `load-path`; both front ends draw in its colours, the GUI's mode line, header line, tab bar and cursor too. `examples/packages/nord-theme.ts` is Nord, as a package that does not ship.
- Indirect buffers (`C-x 4 c`, and Org's `C-c C-x b` for a subtree).
- Fields (C-a and the word commands stop at a prompt's edge).
- Tree-sitter highlighting (`rust-ts-mode`, `js-ts-mode`, `json-ts-mode`, `python-ts-mode`, and `go-ts-mode` with a grammar library), loading the grammars Emacs builds (`libtree-sitter-LANG.dylib` along `treesit-extra-load-path` or in `~/.config/ember/tree-sitter`), and modes' `treesit-font-lock-rules` by `treesit-font-lock-level`.
- Indentation by Emacs's tree-sitter rules.
- The tree for scripts (parsers, nodes, queries).
- Incremental search (`C-s`, `C-r`, `C-M-s`).
- `query-replace` (`M-%`, `C-M-%`).
- `replace-string`.
- `replace-regexp` and the search commands.
- Regular expressions in Emacs's syntax.
- M-x with completion styles (`f-f` completes to `find-file`).
- Completion styles and metadata for packages (`minibuffer.ts`: `defineCompletionStyle` adds to `completion-styles-alist`, `completionAllCompletions` matches the open prompt in its styles, `completingReadWithMetadata` and `completion-metadata-get` carry annotations, groups and sorts).
- Icomplete's list of completions as you type (`icomplete-mode`, on by default, `fido-mode`, `icomplete-vertical-mode`, `fido-vertical-mode`).
- Minibuffer history (`M-p`, `M-n`).
- The minibuffer (a buffer with its prompt as read-only text, `C-x o` out of it and back, and recursive minibuffers with `enable-recursive-minibuffers`).
- `C-h f`, `C-h k` and `C-h b`.
- TypeScript extensions: `init.ts` defines commands and key bindings, `M-x load-file` reloads it, and `M-:` evaluates an expression.

## Packages that do not ship

Ports of packages from outside Emacs, in `examples/packages/`, for a config to copy into `~/.config/ember/packages` and load with `ember.require`, as a config loads them from ELPA or MELPA in Emacs:

- `nord-theme`: the Nord theme (`ember.call("load-theme", "nord", true)`).
- `vundo`: the undo history as a tree (`M-x vundo`: `f`, `b`, `n`, `p`, `a`, `w`, `e`, `l`, `r`, `m`, `u`, `d` for a diff, `RET`, `q`), after vundo 2.4.
- `persistent-scratch`: the scratch buffers kept across sessions (`persistent-scratch-save`, `persistent-scratch-restore`, `persistent-scratch-autosave-mode`, `persistent-scratch-setup-default`, backups), in the file format persistent-scratch.el writes.
- `casual`: Casual's Dired menu (`casual-dired-tmenu`, usually on `?` in Dired), with its menus for sorting, links, changing files, marking by regexp, utilities, searching and settings, after Casual 2.
- `vertico`: every prompt that completes lists its candidates down the minibuffer (`vertico-mode`: `C-n`, `C-p`, `<down>`, `<up>`, `M-<`, `M->`, `TAB`, `RET`, `M-RET`, groups with `M-{` and `M-}`, `vertico-count`, `vertico-cycle`, `vertico-preselect`, sorting by history, length and name), after vertico 2.5.
- `orderless`: the completion style that matches space-separated components in any order, as literals or regexps, with orderless's affix characters and smart case (`completion-styles` `("orderless" "basic")`).
- `marginalia`: annotations beside completion candidates by category (`marginalia-mode`: commands' keys and documentation, variables' values, faces' samples, buffers, files' modes, sizes and ages, bookmarks, minor modes, themes, tabs), and `marginalia-cycle` (`M-A` in a config) to switch annotators.
- `consult`: commands that read with completion and preview where the selection is (`consult-line`, `consult-buffer` with its sources and `b`, `f`, `m`, `p`, `*` and `SPC` narrowing, `consult-buffer-other-window`, `consult-project-buffer`, `consult-recent-file`, `consult-bookmark`, `consult-goto-line`, `consult-outline`, `consult-imenu`, `consult-imenu-multi`, `consult-mark`, `consult-global-mark`, `consult-line-multi`, `consult-register`, `consult-register-load`, `consult-register-store`, `consult-isearch-history`, `consult-yank-pop`, `consult-yank-from-kill-ring`, `consult-history`, `consult-complex-command`, `consult-mode-command`, `consult-ripgrep`, `consult-grep` and `consult-find` searching as you type with `#search#filter`, `consult-compile-error`, `consult-flymake`, `consult-xref` for `xref-show-xrefs-function` and `xref-show-definitions-function`, `consult-theme`), `consult-narrow-key`, `consult-preview-key` and `consultCustomize`.
- `magit` and `magit-section`: Git in a status buffer of sections (`C-x g`, `magit-status`: the branch, upstream, push remote and tag, untracked files, unstaged and staged changes as diffs, stashes, unpushed, unpulled and recent commits; `TAB`, `C-<tab>`, `n`, `p`, `M-n`, `M-p`, `^`, `1` to `4`; `s`, `u`, `k` on a file, hunk or section, `S`, `U`, `RET` to visit, `g`, `$`), with Magit's transient menus, laid out as Magit lays them out: commit (`c`: commit, extend, amend, reword, fixup, squash, alter, augment, revise and instant fixup and squash, the message written in `COMMIT_EDITMSG` with `C-c C-c` and `C-c C-k`, git's own template and hooks through with-editor), push (`P`), pull (`F`), fetch (`f`), branch (`b`, with its configuration), merge (`m`), rebase (`r`: onto the upstream or elsewhere, interactively with the todo list in `git-rebase-mode`, to modify, reword or remove a commit, to autosquash, and continue, skip, edit and abort while one is going on), reset (`X`), stash (`z`), log (`l`, with reflogs and shortlog; `RET` shows a commit), diff (`d`) and dispatch (`h`, `?`: cherry-pick, revert, tag, rebase onto a branch, ignore, init, clone, refs, cherries, remotes, format-patch), after Magit 4; `s` and `u` with a region in a hunk take just its lines.
- `with-editor`: Ember as the editor of the programs it runs, as git's commit messages and rebase todo lists are edited in Ember, with `C-c C-c` and `C-c C-k`, through with-editor's sleeping editor.
- `org-roam`: notes as nodes, files and headings with IDs, in `org-roam-directory` (`org-roam-node-find`, `org-roam-node-insert`, `org-roam-capture` with `org-roam-capture-templates`, `C-c C-c` and `C-c C-k` in the capture buffer, `org-roam-buffer-toggle` for the backlinks of the node at point, `org-roam-db-sync`, `org-roam-db-autosync-mode`, aliases, refs and tags, `org-roam-node-random`, `org-roam-graph` with Graphviz), after org-roam 2.
- `consult-org-roam`: nodes read with consult's preview (`consult-org-roam-mode`), `consult-org-roam-backlinks`, `consult-org-roam-forward-links`, `consult-org-roam-file-find`, `consult-org-roam-search`, and the notes open as an Org-roam group of `consult-buffer`.
